About the Project

The FarmIO project addresses high input costs, soil degradation, and low farmer income caused by excessive chemical use. It promotes a combination of precision farming tools and organic practices through demonstrations and basic monitoring.

The expected outcomes include improved soil health, reduced production costs, higher yields, and enhanced farmer livelihoods through sustainable agriculture practices.

Technology Stack

The scope involves designing and implementing an IoT-based infrastructure to support precision and organic farming practices. It includes deploying sensors for real-time monitoring, automated irrigation systems, and a web-based platform for data visualization and management.
